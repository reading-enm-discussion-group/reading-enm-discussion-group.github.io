To distribute administrative workload, coordination tasks are split into two Coordinator roles held for 6-month terms. Nominations are called every 6 months. Group voting resolves contested roles.

| **Coordinator Role** | **Responsibilities** |
| :---: | :--- |
| **Schedule** | Manages meeting schedule, Signal announcements, topic polling, and Topic Registry. |
| **Venue** | Sources, vets, and books suitable venues; acts as primary venue contact on day of meetings. |

Facilitation is agreed on a session by session basis.

| **Session Facilitator(s)** | Keeps discussion aligned with topic, manages time to 45 minute window, ensures every attendee has an opportunity to participate. |
