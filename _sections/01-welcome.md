We're a peer-led space for discussing the practical application, philosophies, and lived experiences of Polyamory and Ethical Non-Monogamy (ENM) (including perspectives such as Relationship Anarchy).

Our aim is to provide a supportive environment where members can share insights and learn from one another to deepen self-awareness and build healthier relationship practices.
