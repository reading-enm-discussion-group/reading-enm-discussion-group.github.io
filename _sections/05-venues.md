---
title: "Venues"
nav_id: venues
---
Venues selected by the Venue Coordinator should aim to meet the following standards:

- **Acoustics & Layout:** Low background noise (no quiz nights or live sports); layout allows all participants to hear clearly.
- **Accessibility & Transit:** Wheelchair accessible (including step-free toilets); central Reading (RG1).
- **Affordability:** Free or low-cost options; no mandatory food/drink minimum spend.
