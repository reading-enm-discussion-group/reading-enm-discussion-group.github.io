- **Topic submission:** Members can submit topics via an online form at any time. Topics are voted on shortly after each meeting using an online poll to decide the topic for the next meeting. As the Coordinators are also members, they can propose topics too.
- **Topic Registry:** Managed via a shared Google Sheet.
- **Proposer Responsibilities:** Each proposed topic requires a designated Proposer who introduces the topic (5 mins) and provides 2–3 seed questions to facilitate discussion.
- **Session Facilitation:** Each meeting uses a volunteer Session Facilitator. When a topic is selected, the Proposer is offered first refusal to facilitate. If they decline, the Scheduler calls for an opt-in volunteer via Signal at least two weeks prior. First-time facilitators may request a Coordinator as a co-facilitator.
