- **Discussion Ethos:** Discussions explore perspectives, share lived experience, and test ideas rather than debate "correct" relationship styles. Members are encouraged to:
  - ask constructive questions
  - listen to the facilitator and follow directions intended to keep the discussion on topic and to time
  - offer relevant insights with respect for individual differences, differing relational styles and varied levels of experience
  - only share things in the group that don’t violate other people’s privacy or consent.
- **Scope:** This is a discussion group, not a pickup event. Unsolicited romantic or sexual advances in meetings or group chats are not permitted.
- **Behaviour expectations:** Respect personal boundaries, maintain confidentiality regarding personal stories shared, and use respectful language.
- **Own boundaries:** Members are encouraged to communicate their boundaries and seek peer-to-peer resolution for minor communication clashes.
- **Handling Disruptive Behavior:**
  - _In-Session:_ Facilitators and coordinators can pause discussions, address violations directly, or ask disruptive individuals to leave.
  - _Private Reporting:_ Members can report boundary violations privately to any coordinator.
  - _Removal:_ repeated or serious violations will result in permanent removal from meetings and digital spaces. Handled by Consensus of the two Coordinators.
